## 2. Critical Free-Tier Analysis & Transparency Warnings

### Warning A: Render Free Container Spin-Down & Ephemeral Disk
- **Behavior:** Render free web services spin down (sleep) after 15 minutes of zero web traffic.
- **Wake-up Latency:** The next visitor will experience a 45–60 second delay while the container boots.
- **Disk Ephemerality:** Free container filesystems are ephemeral. Any files written to `backend/uploads/` or local `knowledge_bridge.db` are wiped when the container spins down or redeploys!
- **Mitigation:**
  1. The app auto-seeds initial demonstration projects (`KB-APOLLO`, `KB-FINPAY`) on startup so it is never blank.
  2. For permanent zero-cost persistence in the cloud, pair the app with **Neon Free PostgreSQL** (see Section 4).

### Warning B: Render Free PostgreSQL 30-Day Expiry Notice
- **Critical Limitation:** Render offers a "Free PostgreSQL" database, but **it expires and is permanently deleted after 30 days** unless upgraded to a paid $7/month plan.
- **Policy Decision:** We explicitly **DO NOT recommend Render PostgreSQL** for zero-budget projects.
- **Solution:** Use **Neon Serverless PostgreSQL (`neon.tech`)** which offers a permanent free tier with 0.5 GB storage and does NOT expire after 30 days.

### Warning C: Google Gemini Free Tier Quota Limits
- **Limits:** 15 Requests Per Minute (RPM) and 1,500 Requests Per Day (RPD).
- **Graceful Degradation:** If free quota is exceeded (HTTP 429), Knowledge Bridge AI **automatically switches to the Zero-Cost Rule-Based Extractor**.
- **Transparency:** The system marks fallback items with `is_demo_mode: True` and `is_ai_generated: False`. It never falsely claims rule-based findings were generated by live AI.
- **Zero Billing:** No credit card is ever requested or charged.

## 3. Option 1: One-Click Free Deployment on Render (Web Service)

Render Web Services are 100% free with no credit card required.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Push this repository to your **GitHub Free** account.
2. Sign up at [Render](https://render.com) using your GitHub account (No credit card required).
3. In the Render Dashboard, click **New +** ➔ **Web Service**.
4. Connect your GitHub repository.
5. Configure the service settings:
   - **Name:** `knowledge-bridge-ai`
   - **Environment:** `Docker`
   - **Region:** `Oregon (US West)` or closest free region
   - **Instance Type:** `Free` (0.1 CPU, 512 MB RAM, ₹0.00/month)
   - **Health Check Path:** `/api/health`
6. Under **Environment Variables**, add:
   - `PORT`: `8000`
   - `GEMINI_API_KEY`: *(Optional)* Your free key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey)
   - `DATABASE_URL`: *(Optional)* Your permanent free Neon Postgres URL (see Section 4)
7. Click **Create Web Service**.
8. Once built, your application is live with free managed HTTPS at `https://<your-app-name>.onrender.com`.

*(Note: Per user instructions, deployment requires explicit user approval before execution).*
